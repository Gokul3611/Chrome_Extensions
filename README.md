# Simple English Dictionary (Brave Extension)

---

## 🔄 How to Reload in Brave

1. Open `brave://extensions` in Brave.
2. Click the **Reload (↻)** icon on **Simple English Dictionary**.

---

## ⚡ How to Use

* **Double-Click**: Double-click any word on any webpage to see its definition.
* **Highlight / Drag**: Highlight/select any word with your mouse, and the definition window pops up immediately.
* **Right-Click**: Right-click any selected word and choose **Define "[word]"**.
* **Toolbar Icon**: Click the extension icon in Brave's top-right toolbar to search any word manually.
* **Dismiss**: Press `Esc`, click `✕`, or click anywhere outside.
